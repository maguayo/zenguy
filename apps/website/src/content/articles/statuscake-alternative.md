StatusCake is one of the older names in website monitoring: uptime, page speed, SSL, some contact options, plans that grow with monitor count. It is a fine ping tool. It is not a checkout walker.

If you searched “StatusCake alternative” because the bill or the UI aged, you have many ping peers (UptimeRobot, Hyperping, Better Stack). If you searched it because **customers found a broken flow StatusCake called up**, that is Zenguy's lane.

## What StatusCake is good at

- HTTP(s) uptime from multiple locations;
- page-speed style checks on some plans;
- SSL expiry;
- a familiar, check-count-based price.

Re-read their current pricing before you compare dollars. Like Pingdom, the product is built around **URLs**, not goals.

## What it will not tell you

The same list as every ping vendor:

- the button is in the HTML and does nothing;
- the total is 0,00 €;
- mobile hides pay;
- checkout lives on another host that is also “up”.

Page speed going from 1.2s to 1.9s is a useful graph. It is not an incident about revenue.

## When to keep StatusCake

You like the speed reports, the SSL reminders, and the locations. Keep them. Add a flow watcher.

## When to switch the “website monitoring” tab in your head

When the outage you fear is a **working page with a dead funnel**. Then the alternative is not a slightly cheaper StatusCake. It is a real browser, a paragraph of English, and an alert that quotes the total.
